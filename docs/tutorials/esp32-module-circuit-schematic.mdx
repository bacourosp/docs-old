## Overview

In this tutorial (inspired by the *Hardware Academy Custom ESP32 PCB* workflow and [`seveibar/wifi-test-board-1`](https://tscircuit.com/seveibar/wifi-test-board-1)), we will design a complete, schematic-focused **ESP32-WROOM-32E development board** in **tscircuit** (`routingDisabled`).

To make the board easy and inexpensive to assemble at **JLCPCB**, we replace hard-to-source ICs with high-availability JLCPCB stock equivalents:

| Subsystem | Part Number | JLCPCB Part # | Why This Chip? |
|-----------|-------------|---------------|----------------|
| **Wi-Fi / BLE MCU Module** | `ESP32-WROOM-32E (16MB)` | `C701342` | Integrated `40MHz` crystal, SPI flash, and PCB trace antenna (no RF matching network required) |
| **3.3V LDO Regulator** | `AMS1117-3.3` | `C6186` | Basic library part (`SOT-223`), delivers up to `1A` continuous for Wi-Fi TX bursts |
| **USB-to-UART Bridge** | `CH340C` | `C84681` | Built-in `12MHz` clock generator (eliminates external crystal and load capacitors) |
| **USB-C Receptacle** | `TYPE-C-31-M-12` | `C165948` | Standard 16-pin USB 2.0 Type-C connector with `5.1kΩ` `CC1`/`CC2` pull-down resistors |
| **Auto-Boot / Reset Dual NPN** | `UMH3N` / `2x S8050` | `C2146` | Classic NodeMCU DTR/RTS truth-table circuit for automatic `esptool.py` flashing |
| **Schottky Power Diode** | `SS34` / `1N5819WS` | `C191023` | Prevents `5V` back-feeding into the host USB port |

## Key Design Decisions Explained

### 1. Why `CH340C` Instead of `CP2102N`?
In many reference tutorials, the Silicon Labs `CP2102` QFN-28 chip is used, which has higher JLCPCB extended-part assembly fees and tight thermal-pad soldering tolerances. Swapping to the **WCH `CH340C` (`SOIC-16`, JLCPCB `#C84681`)**:
- Eliminates the external `12MHz` crystal and two `22pF` load capacitors.
- Runs directly from `3.3V` (`VCC` tied to `V3` with `100nF` decoupling) so `TXD`/`RXD` logic levels natively match the `ESP32-WROOM-32E` without level shifters.

### 2. USB-C `5.1kΩ` CC1/CC2 Pull-Downs (`R1`, `R2`)
A common mistake on custom ESP32 boards is leaving `CC1` and `CC2` floating on the USB-C receptacle (`J1`), which prevents USB-C PD chargers and MacBooks from supplying `5V` on `VBUS`. Adding two independent `5.1kΩ` resistors (`R1` on `CC1` and `R2` on `CC2` to `GND`) advertises the board as a standard `5V` USB sink.

### 3. RC Delay on `EN` (`R3 = 10kΩ`, `C5 = 100nF`)
Per Espressif's datasheet, the `EN` (`CHIP_PU`) pin must rise *after* the `3.3V` rail stabilizes. The `10kΩ` pull-up (`R3`) combined with the `100nF` capacitor (`C5`) provides a `~1 ms` RC time constant for reliable power-on reset.
